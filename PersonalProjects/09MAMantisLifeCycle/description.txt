MA: Mantis Life Cycle
---
A poster of a praying mantis's life cycle, from my MA in Illustration. The task was to show one life cycle of our choice, and every stage had to be drawn by hand, in pencil, on paper. So the five stages, from the egg case to an adult laying a new one, are pencil drawings with no digital painting.

Then I scanned them and put the poster together digitally.
