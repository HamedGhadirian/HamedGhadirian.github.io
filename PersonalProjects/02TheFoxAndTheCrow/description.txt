The Fox And The Crow
---
I painted this digitally in Photoshop and tried it in different lighting. I liked the night version most, so I took it into Blender, split it into layers and animated them into a short loop. It also became a live wallpaper: [Wallpaper Engine version](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2848558348).

Later, I painted the same fable again as a children's book page for my MA: [MA: The Fox and the Crow](/PersonalProjects/08MATheFoxAndTheCrow/thefoxandthecrow.html).
