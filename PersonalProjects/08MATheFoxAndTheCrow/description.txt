MA: The Fox and the Crow
---
A page for a children's book of The Fox and the Crow, from my MA in Illustration. I made the snowy forest in 3D first and rendered it. Then I painted the fox and the crow on top, and painted over the render too.

The other two pages continue the story, but they aren't finished.

I painted this fable once before, before my MA: [The Fox and the Crow](/PersonalProjects/02TheFoxAndTheCrow/thefoxandthecrow.html).
