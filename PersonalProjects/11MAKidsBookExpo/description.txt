MA: Kids' Book Expo
---
A poster illustration for a children's book exhibition, from my MA in Illustration. The brief was open. I drew a boy reading on the floor, and on the wall behind him, his shadow turns into an archer. The archer is a famous character in Iranian folklore, Arash.
