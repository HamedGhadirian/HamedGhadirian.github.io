MA: Kids' Book Expo
---
A poster illustration for a children's book exhibition, from my MA in Illustration. The brief was open. I drew a boy reading on the floor, and on the wall behind him, his shadow turns into an archer.

Like my [Frankenstein](/PersonalProjects/10MAFrankenstein/frankenstein.html) cover, the story is told through a shadow.
