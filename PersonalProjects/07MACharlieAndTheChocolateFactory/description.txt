MA: Charlie and the Chocolate Factory
---
An illustration for Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, from my MA in Illustration. The task was to illustrate two books, one for children and one for teenagers. This was my teen book; the children's one was [The Fox and the Crow](/PersonalProjects/08MATheFoxAndTheCrow/thefoxandthecrow.html).

At first we had to make three frames for each book, so I sketched three ideas. Later the task changed to one frame per book, and I took the first idea to the end: Charlie running down a snowy street, with the factory waiting at the end of it.
