Stylized Cassette Player
---
My own design, from my sketch to the final piece: modelling, sculpting, texturing, rigging and a short animation. It's probably the best thing I've made so far.

It started as a record player and ended up as a cassette player. While designing it, I kept imagining the world it belongs to: a slightly steampunk, sci-fi place, and how someone there would build and use a device like this. It was my first step into stylized, steampunk design.

If I made it again today, I would change a lot. But I still love it.
---
Blender, ZBrush, Substance Painter
