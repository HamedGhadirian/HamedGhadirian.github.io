Stylized Dire and Radiant Package
---
A stylized environment pack for Unreal Engine 5 that I made and sold myself. My main inspiration was Dota 2, along with the stylized worlds of many modern games. I wanted the assets to work from both a top-down and a third-person camera.

It was sold on the Unreal Marketplace and is now available on BlenderKit as two sets: [Stylized Dire](https://www.blenderkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/770f5fe2-47f2-4628-b48c-ac9e2612e84e/) and [Stylized Radiant](https://www.blenderkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/9c8a5519-33f2-45c5-98c2-ebb75605a792/). It took a long time, and today I can see a lot I would optimise. But it was my first full pack, and it taught me what a buyer needs: clean setup, collision, LODs and flexible materials.

-Unique meshes: 48
-Collision: yes, custom
-LODs: yes, auto
-Materials: 7 materials, 4 VFX materials, 84 material instances, plus 37 instances for the demo (mostly using RVT, a virtual texture that blends assets into the landscape)
-Textures: 76, at 1k and 2k
---
Blender, ZBrush, Substance Painter, UE5
