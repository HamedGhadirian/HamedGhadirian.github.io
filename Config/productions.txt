3D Artist
Nepox Inc · Freelance, full-time, remote
Apr 2024 – Feb 2026
/Images/Experience/Nepox-Routers.webp
A real-time 3D app for Android, made in Unreal Engine, that shows your home network and home security as a 3D house. I joined as the 3D artist, and over time I worked on the app's design too.
I modelled about 80 devices, like phones, laptops and modems, with 226 colour and screen variants. They had to be brand-neutral: easy to recognise, with no real brand or logo, so each one needed its own simple design.
I also built about 30 rooms that fit together into the app's isometric house. We redesigned the whole house around how the app works, not just how it looks: every room has placement tags, so the developer's system can put each device in the right spot.
I designed and built the app's whole UI, including 3D data views like a speed test and a Wi-Fi analyzer. Each view went back and forth between me and the programmer. When a design was hard to explain, I made a simple Blueprint prototype to show how it reacts to live values.
Setting up hundreds of variants by hand was slow, so I made Blueprint editor tools to edit them in bulk. I also made the marketing renders and promo videos, like the one in this picture.
